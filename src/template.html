Your survival crew

How survival is scored
  • Every character has a hidden score for each of the six roles. Iron Man makes a better Brains than a Medic. Put people where they shine.
  • Leader counts the most. A strong leader also keeps hotheads in line.
  • Your weakest link drags everyone down. One empty-handed Medic can sink a team of legends.
  • Personalities matter. Some characters bring the whole group's morale up. Big egos clash. Lone wolves hate group roles. And not everyone in a movie is on the hero's side.
  • Teammates from the same franchise work better together. A crew from all five genres earns a crossover bonus.
  • Six spins, two reshoots. Same crew, same odds, every time. It's supposed to be hard.
